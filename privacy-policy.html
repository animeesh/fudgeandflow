Privacy Policy


1. Information We Collect

1. Personal Information: When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your name and email address to send you updates, promotions, and news about our café.

2. Automatic Data Collection: We may collect certain data automatically through your use of our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. This helps us improve the functionality of the website and provide a better user experience.

3. Tracking Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to monitor how users interact with our website. This includes tracking how visitors navigate the site, the pages they visit, and how long they stay on those pages.

2. How We Use Your Information

1. Newsletter Subscription: We use the information you provide to send you our newsletter, including updates, offers, and news related to our café.

2. Tracking & Analytics: We use tracking tools to monitor user behavior on the website. This helps us analyze user engagement, improve website content, and enhance overall user experience. We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to collect data about site interactions.

3. Marketing: We may use tracking data to serve you targeted ads or promotions based on your interactions with our website.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

1. Cookies: Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help us track your preferences, improve user experience, and analyze website traffic. You can control the use of cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the website may not work without them.

2. Web Beacons: These are small graphic images that track user interactions with our website. They help us understand how effective our content is and how users are engaging with it.

4. Sharing of Your Information

1. We do not share or sell your personal information to third parties except to service providers who assist us in managing the newsletter or provide analytics services. These providers may include email service providers or analytics platforms.

2. Third-Party Analytics: We may share anonymized data with third-party analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) to help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

5. Security

We use encryption and secure protocols to protect your personal data. However, no system is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee the complete security of your information.

6. Your Rights

1. Unsubscribe: You have the right to unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send.

2. Data Access & Deletion: You may request access to the personal data we hold about you or ask us to delete it by contacting us at Fudgeandflow@gmail.com.

3. Cookie Management: You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may limit the functionality of certain website features.

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the date at the top of this page.
